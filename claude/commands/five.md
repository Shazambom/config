## The Five Whys Technique

Start with the problem and ask "why" repeatedly until you reach the root cause.

### Problem Statement
Clearly state the problem being investigated.

### Why #1
Why is this happening? (First-level cause)

### Why #2
Why is that the case? (Dig deeper)

### Why #3
Why does that occur? (Keep going)

### Why #4
Why is that true? (Getting closer)

### Why #5
Why is that happening? (Root cause)

## Analysis

- **Root Cause**: What is the fundamental issue?
- **Contributing Factors**: What else played a role?
- **Systemic Issues**: Is this a pattern or one-off?

## Recommendations

1. **Immediate Fix**: What stops the bleeding now?
2. **Root Cause Fix**: What prevents this permanently?
3. **Process Change**: What prevents similar issues in the future?

Note: You may need fewer or more than 5 "whys" - stop when you reach an actionable root cause.
