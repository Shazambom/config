---
description: Review your plan and identify assumptions or gaps
---

Review your current plan and evaluate it critically:

1. **Assumptions**: What assumptions have been made? Are they valid?
2. **Gaps**: What information is missing or unclear?
3. **Risks**: What could go wrong with this approach?
4. **Scope**: Does the plan match the original request?

If you identify gaps or unclear requirements, ask clarifying questions before proceeding.
