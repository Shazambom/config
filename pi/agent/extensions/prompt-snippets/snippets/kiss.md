Apply KISS: Keep it simple, stupid. Make simplicity a design goal. Choose the simplest design that meets the requirements. Avoid unnecessary abstractions, dependencies, configuration, and speculative features. Prefer clear, direct code that is easy to understand and maintain. Do not sacrifice correctness or safety for fewer lines.
