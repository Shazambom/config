Use online research as an escape hatch when you've spent too long making little progress or tried many approaches without success. Search from several angles and levels of abstraction: the exact symptom, the underlying mechanism, and the broader problem. Look for existing solutions and other people's reasoning. Treat results as references and suggested paths, not authority. Verify promising ideas against the actual system and requirements; don't let them cloud your judgment, change the end goal, or expand scope. Never put secrets, private code, or internal data into searches.
